# Lesson — a text heuristic must be handed whole thoughts, never physical lines — hard wrap…

**Trigger:** distilling this repo's history produced lesson Triggers cut off mid-clause on a dangling "the" and others that began mid-sentence, because a wrapped bullet was scored one physical line at a time

**Insight:** a text heuristic must be handed whole thoughts, never physical lines — hard wrapping turns a line-at-a-time scan into a fragment generator, and a fragment that clears every semantic filter is indistinguishable from a finding

**Rejected:** widening the refusal to every labelled bullet — a real observation carries a component label too ("<pkg>: 4 of 21 lookups returned nothing"), and the label is not the tell; what follows it is

**Keywords:** distill, trigger, symptom, hard-wrap, scavenger, fragment, ADR-0028
